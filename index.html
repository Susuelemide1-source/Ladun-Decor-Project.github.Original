  • Ladun Decor Analysis

    This is a brief description and our goals for ladun decor. Ladun decor is one of the biggest exterior and interior decorator globally. Recently, they have been facing multiple challenges with their booking system due to the increase in demand for their services. This was discovered after analysing their booking system and the data required from their customers. The goal of this project is to provide a solution to their booking system and improve their customer service. We will be working closely with their team to implement a new booking system that addresses these challenges and enhance their overall customer experience. This analysis was perform using Microsoft Excel.
